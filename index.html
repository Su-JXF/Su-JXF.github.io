Research & projects

01

RESEARCH · DEC 2025 — PRESENT

Agent Memory

An agent-memory system for longitudinal user modeling, designed to track evolving user states across multi-turn interactions. On ES-MemEval p1, the GPT-5 configuration reached 31.96% F1, 61.17% BERTScore, and 1.570/2 LLM-as-Judge, improving on the reported GPT-4o baseline.

Student Researcher · Advisor: Yangbin Chen

02

LLM AGENTS · JUN — AUG 2026

Emotion-Conditioned Generation

Investigated emotion-conditioned generation across DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, and Qwen models and helped redesign a 10-category emotion taxonomy. Across 411 blind-evaluated turns, response-stance agreement rose from 71.1% to 74.7%, and interaction agency from 57.4% to 61.3%.

Large Language Model Algorithm Intern · H3C Group

03

NLP · SEP — NOV 2025

Fake News Detection

Built and evaluated a BERT-based stance classification pipeline on the FNC-1 benchmark, achieving 0.918 accuracy and 87.7% of the maximum benchmark score.

Research Participant · Project Mentor: Di Jin
